# Journey Design 2.0 preview

The photo-first redesign proposed on 7 Nov 2026: real photos, bold Plus Jakarta Sans, glass controls that float over the content, light and dark themes, and three accent colours to choose from (Iris, Lagoon, Sun).

- **View it:** [Journey Design 2.0](https://claude.ai/artifact/Hmc5sAXD5cRtgof87L354X) (claude.ai, private to the owner).
- **Source:** `index.html` is the page as published. It's an HTML fragment: claude.ai adds the `<html>`, `<head>` and `<body>` wrapper when it publishes the page.
- **Screens:** Welcome, Home, Journey, Entry, Video clip, Challenge, Map, Add entry.
- **Tokens:** the `:root`, `.app[data-accent=…]` and `.ph` blocks at the top of the stylesheet are the colour, type and shape tokens to carry into Figma variables and the app's theme file.

Photos are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/license), via [Lorem Picsum](https://picsum.photos); the photographers are credited at the bottom of the page. People, names and stories in the screens are made up.
