JourneyDesign direction 2.0 · Nov 2026

Real photos, bold type, glass controls.

A photo-first redesign of the Journey app. It borrows the clean, friendly feel of the Airbnb screens you shared and adds a dark theme, glass controls that float over the photos, and a choice of three accent colours. Switch the theme and try each colour; every screen below updates.

What changed from the first version

Drawn landscapes standing in for photosReal, full-bleed photography and video clips
Bookish serif titles on cream paperBold Plus Jakarta Sans on clean white or deep black
Flat bars stuck to the screen edgesA glass tab bar and buttons that float over the content
One warm terracottaThree vivid accents to choose from: Iris, Lagoon or Sun
Light first, dark as an extraLight and dark designed together, following the phone setting
Thin outlined cardsSoft Airbnb-style cards, 3D icon tabs and big rounded photos

The design system behind it

The same tokens drive every screen above, so picking a colour or a theme changes the whole app at once. These become Figma variables and the app's theme file.

Accent colour

Pick one. Buttons, the + tab, progress rings and milestones use it.

Surfaces

Neutral greys with a faint cool tint, so photos stay the most colourful thing on screen.

Type

One family, Plus Jakarta Sans, in five weights. Big titles are set tight at ExtraBold.
