Community guidelines

Journey is for telling a story as it happens and cheering other people on. A few things don't belong here.

Not on Journey

  • Hate and harassment. No attacks on people for who they are, no threats, no bullying, no piling on.
  • Nudity and sexual content. Beach and swimwear photos are fine. Explicit content is not.
  • Violence and dangerous acts. No graphic violence, and nothing that encourages people to hurt themselves or others.
  • Pretending to be someone else. Be yourself, or be clearly a fan or parody account.
  • Spam and scams. No selling, no fake giveaways, no posting the same thing over and over.
  • Other people's private details. Don't post someone's address, documents or private messages.
  • Anything illegal where you are or where it is shown.

What happens when something is reported

Anyone can report a journey, an entry, a comment or a person. A report is private and a person reads each one. Until they have, a reported journey is left out of Discover and search. If it breaks these guidelines it is removed; if it doesn't, nothing changes.

The automatic check

Every photo is checked when it is uploaded. If the check has doubts, the photo is hidden from everyone but you until a person has looked at it. You are told, and you can ask for a second look. A comment with certain words is held the same way.
