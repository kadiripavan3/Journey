Privacy

What we keep, why, who sees it, and how you get rid of it.

What we keep

  • Your sign-in: your email address, or the identifier Apple or Google gives us. We never see a password.
  • Your profile: name, username, picture, bio, link and the interests you picked.
  • What you post: journeys, entries, photos, clips, comments and likes, and who you follow.
  • Places: the place you put on an entry. Location data inside a photo is removed when the photo is uploaded. A clip is converted by our video service before anyone can watch it; people get the converted clip, never the file your phone sent.
  • Your settings: which notifications you want, and the people you have blocked.
  • Reports: what you reported and why. The person you report is not told who reported them.

Who sees what

A public journey can be seen by anyone, also on the web. An unlisted journey can be seen by anyone who has its link. A private journey can be seen only by the people who write in it; someone you invite to write in it sees its title, cover and authors before they answer. Your profile shows your public journeys. When you block someone, neither of you sees the other in the app. A public journey's web page stays open to anyone who isn't signed in; make a journey private to close it.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, we don't show advertising, and we don't track where you are. There is no live location.

Who helps us run Journey

Other companies store and deliver parts of the service for us: the database and sign-in, storage for photos and clips, video delivery, notifications, email, and the automatic photo check. They handle your data only to do that work. The full list is added here before launch.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Deleting an entry, a journey or your account removes it from Journey at once and for good; the photo and clip files follow within minutes, and copies that the delivery network keeps to load photos quickly are gone within a day. Photos you uploaded but never used are removed after a week at most.

A report you send, and the record of what a reviewer decided about it, is kept for six months. If a reviewer removes a comment, its words are kept in that record for the same time. A request to delete an account made on the web is kept, without the address it came from, for a month after it was handled. If we remove an account for breaking the community guidelines, we keep a fingerprint of the address it signed in with (not the address itself), so that address can't be used to sign up again.

Children

Journey is not for children under 13 (16 where the law of your country says so). If you believe a child is using it, tell us and we will remove the account.
