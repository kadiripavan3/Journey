Delete your account

The quickest way is in the app: Settings, Account, Delete account. Your profile and everything you posted are gone at once; the photo files follow within minutes. If you can't open the app any more, ask here.

What is deleted

Your profile, journeys, entries, photos, clips, comments, likes and follows. For good: it can't be undone.

Ask us to delete it

We write to the address you give, to make sure the request comes from you, and delete the account once you answer.
